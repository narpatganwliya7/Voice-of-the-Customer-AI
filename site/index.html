Every review, heard.

Écoute is an AI agent that reads what customers of 41 French brands write on Google Play and the App Store, finds what costs each brand its stars, and tells the right team what to fix first.

Explore a brandWatch the agent investigate
Designed and built byNarpat Ganwliya

From a one-star rant to a ranked fix list.

Four steps run on their own in GitHub Actions. Every review is scored by a local model at no cost; the AI analyst steps in only when the numbers flag something that needs explaining.

For every review

Collection, personal-data masking, the local classifier (15 topics, tone, intent to leave, severity) and the SQL models. No API calls, seconds per run.

Only when it matters

Complaint spikes, releases that landed badly and the biggest causes of lost stars go to the AI analyst, which investigates with SQL and real reviews and files a brief.

Caught the day it breaks, not the quarter after.

Each app version is compared with the one before it, and each day's complaints with the brand's own 28-day baseline. These alerts are what wake the AI analyst.

An agent that investigates, cites and recommends.

For each alert, the analyst queries the data with SQL, reads real reviews and files a brief with owners and actions. Every step is logged, and the reviews it cites are checked against what it actually read.

What is measured, and what is not.

    Limits

    • App reviews over-represent strong opinions: a signal, not a survey.
    • Stars lost come from a regression: correlation, not proof of cause.
    • Topic labels come from a free AI annotator distilled into a local model; quality figures are shown, not assumed.
    • Capture delay is reported only from live runs that measured it.
