Always-on Voice of the Customer

écoute

Every review, captured within minutes. Every issue, explained. Every fix, prioritised.

Écoute reads what customers of France's best-known brands write on Google Play and the App Store, turns each review into a structured signal, and ranks what to fix first by the stars it costs.

How Écoute listens

From a one-star rant to a ranked fix list, automatically.

Four stages run on their own in GitHub Actions. The fast lane scores every review with a local model for free; the deep lane calls an AI analyst only when the numbers flag something worth a human-quality explanation.

Fast lane · every review

Collector → PII masking → local classifier (15 topics, tone, churn intent, severity) → SQL models. No API calls, no cost, seconds per run.

Deep lane · only what matters

Complaint spikes, releases that landed badly and the biggest drivers of lost stars go to the AI analyst, which investigates with SQL and real reviews and files a brief.

Release radar & spikes

Caught the day it breaks, not the quarter after.

Each app version is compared with the one before it, and each day's complaints with the brand's own 28-day baseline. These are the alerts that wake the AI analyst.

The AI analyst

An agent that investigates, cites, and recommends.

For each alert, Écoute Analyst queries the data with SQL, reads real reviews and files a brief with owners and actions. Every step is logged below; cited reviews are checked against what the agent actually read.

Method & honesty

What is measured, and what is not.

Data

    Model quality

    Limits

    • App reviews over-represent strong opinions; they are a signal, not a survey.
    • Stars-lost figures come from a regression: correlation, not proof of cause.
    • Topic labels come from a free AI annotator distilled into a local model; quality figures are shown, not assumed.
    • Capture latency is reported only from live runs that measured it.
