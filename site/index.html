Every review, priced in stars.

Écoute reads what customers of 41 French brands write on Google Play and the App Store, works out how many stars each problem costs, and sends an AI agent to investigate when something breaks.

How it works.

Four stages run in GitHub Actions. Every review is scored by a local model at no cost; the AI agent steps in only when the numbers flag something that needs explaining.

What is measured, and what is not.

Data

    Model quality

    Limits

    • App reviews over-represent strong opinions: a signal, not a survey.
    • Stars lost come from a regression: correlation, not proof of cause.
    • Topic labels come from a free AI annotator distilled into a local model; quality figures are shown, not assumed.
    • The site is a snapshot; capture delay is reported only from live runs that measured it.
